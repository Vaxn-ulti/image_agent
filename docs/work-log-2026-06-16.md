- Added `--require-completed-task` to `apps/api/scripts/smoke_remote_agent.py`.
- When enabled, strict smoke fetches `/tasks/{task_id}` and fails unless the task is `completed`, matches the supplied project/task ids, has a positive series id, and reports a privacy-safe workflow type.
